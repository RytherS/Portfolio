There's a lot more to me than just "software engineer".

In general, I am kind, curious, and get along well with just about everyone! If culture fit is as important to you as it is to me though, allow me to show you a bit more of who I am outside of professional work.

Going to school at Neumont College of Computer Science was one of the best decisions I ever made. Of course I learned a lot about programming and problem-solving, but arguably more importantly, I learned to have an open mind and an appreciation for diversity.

The people I had the pleasure of meeting and learning from were by far the most impactful aspect of my college experience.

I love being outdoors. Whether it is horseback riding through the incredible redwood forests of California or just sprucing up the flowerbeds in my front yard, it quiets my mind and helps me reflect on the beauty of simplicity and the importance of preserving the natural world.

If you haven't already done it today (or even if you have), I encourage you to go get some fresh air!

I grew up with motorcycles and have always been thrilled by the experience of zooming along on two wheels. When I was a kid, I would race dirt bikes with my brother and cousins around our yard that we turned into a full-blown mini racetrack! Riding motorcycles has taught me that practice and dedication lead to confidence and consistency.

I currently ride a 2025 Honda SCL500 that I have stuck a couple of modifications on to be able to take it out camping!

Video games are how I got into software development! The incredible blend of artistry and technology that goes into creating immersive experiences is what drew me to programming. Growing up with games like Pokemon and The Legend of Zelda bolstered my appreciation for attention to detail and even helped me learn to read!

While I still enjoy and revisit those kinds of classic Nintendo titles, nowadays I tend to play more challenging games like Resident Evil (both modern and classic!), Dark Souls, Baldur's Gate, Project Zomboid, etc.
